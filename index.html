Common questions

Before you call

What should I bring to my first consultation?

Any documents related to your matter: court papers, contracts, deeds, insurance letters or existing wills. A short timeline of events helps too. Don't worry if you don't have everything; we'll tell you what's needed.

How do your fees work?

It depends on the matter. Some work is billed hourly, some has a flat fee, and many personal injury cases are handled on a contingency basis. We explain fees in writing before any work begins.

Can you help if I live outside West Virginia?

We regularly work with clients across Pennsylvania, Maryland, West Virginia and Virginia. Tell us where your matter is and we'll let you know how we can help, or point you to someone who can.

How quickly will I hear back?

We return calls and consultation requests within one business day. If your matter is time-sensitive, please call the office directly.
